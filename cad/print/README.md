# Print files

> **Start with [`cad/README.md`](../README.md): it lists the exact files of the machine as it runs today.**
> This page is the revision-by-revision history of the print folders, kept as it was written during the build,
> so its first lines describe an older state.

**Print from `rev-j_CURRENT/`, plus the rev k add-on parts in `rev-k_CURRENT/`.** Older revisions live in `*_superseded/` folders for reference; never print from them.
When a new revision lands, its folder becomes `rev-X_CURRENT/` (the complete set) and the files it replaces move to `rev-<old>_superseded/`.

**Rev k (2026-11-09) is pending parts:** the rim bearing (6820ZZ, 100 × 125 × 13) and 4 longer tie rods are on order. Rev k replaces no printed part, it only adds the six below (report `docs/2026-11-09-rev-k-rim-bearing.md`), so `rev-j_CURRENT/` stays the build set and **nothing moves to `*_superseded/` until Brian confirms the bearing fits**. The one change to printed hardware is four Ø4.0 holes drilled through the rev j shell's deck (the collar is the template).

**Rev o (2026-11-20) builds on rev n as you built it** (clamp ring out, drive 3.2 mm lower): cup v2, a centring jig v2 (the rev n jig no longer reaches the as-built hub's teeth), fit coupons, and option D (slip ring fixed under the deck in a bracket, driven by a printed mast; brass contacts in the hub's crown; Mill-Max pogo pins in nut plate v2). Print `rev-o_CURRENT/` in its README's order; report `docs/2026-11-20-rev-o-cad.md`.

**Rev n (2026-11-17) replaces rev m.2's drive spool and lead guide** (the spool broke twice during assembly): print `rev-n_CURRENT/` for the drive. From `rev-m_CURRENT/revm2_drive_parts_PET-CF_plate` only the clamp ring (now the cup's seat) and the motor hanger are still used; do not print its spool or lead guide again.

**Rev m (2026-11-13) is the base-power add-on on top of rev k:** slip ring on the axis, motor on a printed hanger driving a printed 40T spool through a GT2 belt, bank out, centred sled v2 (report `docs/2026-11-13-rev-m-cad.md`). Its files are in `rev-m_CURRENT/`; the rev l / rev j sled and the Oldham dog + disc are not needed if you build rev m.

Each part has `.3mf` (project, open in Bambu Studio; filament 1 = black), `.gcode.3mf` (sliced, send directly) and a local `.plate_1.gcode` (gitignored).
The PET-CF bottom-cap plate and top cap are sliced from `cad/rotor_bottom_cap_f.stl` / `cad/rotor_top_cap.stl` with `--filament "Fiberon PET-CF @BBL H2D" --process "process/0.20mm Strength PET-CF @BBL H2D.json" --extruder 1 --bed "Textured PEI Plate" --tag PET-CF --set wall_loops=6 --set top_shell_layers=6 --set bottom_shell_layers=6 --set sparse_infill_density=85%` (Bambu Studio refuses gyroid at 100 %). The sled and base-shell files are sliced by `fusionclaude/slicer/slice.py` from `cad/rotor_sled_j.stl` / `cad/base_shell_j.stl` (`--filament "Bambu PETG Matte @BBL H2D 0.4 nozzle" --process "process/0.20mm Structural PETG-PLA @BBL H2D.json" --extruder 1 --bed "Textured PEI Plate" --tag PETG`; the draft with `"Bambu PLA Matte @BBL H2D"` and `"process/0.20mm PLA Fitcheck @BBL H2D.json"`), and the post positions were measured back from the exported mesh. Before slicing, every part is run through the floating-island check `fusionclaude/scripts/151_pov9_island_check.py` (0.2 mm layers; flags any region with no contact with the layer below): all rev-j_CURRENT parts pass.
