== D. SKF frictional moment (M = phi_ish phi_rs G_rr (nu n)^0.6 + G_sl mu_sl + M_seal), N mm = mN m
  Axial load on the 6820: ring + plate 2.0 N, full rotor 8.7 N, + battery 10.6 N; Fr 1 N
  6820 rolling + sliding (no seal), mN m:      150 rpm   188 rpm   225 rpm   250 rpm
    run-in grease, nu 150 (full rotor)          34.7     38.9     42.3     44.4
    run-in grease, nu 250 (full rotor)          44.7     49.3     52.9     55.0
    light oil nu 15 (full rotor)                 9.6     10.9     12.1     12.8
    light oil nu 30 (full rotor)                14.3     16.2     18.0     19.0
    light oil nu 30 (+ battery)                 15.5     17.7     19.5     20.7
    light oil nu 30 (ring + plate)               7.7      8.8      9.8     10.4
  If the 6820 had contact seals (SKF RS1 row, D > 100: 0.018 x 106.6^2.25): M_seal = 657 mN m. Yours is ZZ: shields don't touch, and removing them changed nothing (11-22).
  The hub's 6802-2RS (RS1 row, D <= 62: 0.023 x 17.9^2.25 + 2): M_seal = 17.2 mN m -- now the largest single drag in the drive
  Fresh factory fill, measured by difference: 100-130 mN m at ~142 rpm, vs the model's run-in 37 mN m: x2.7-3.5 for churning a full, fresh fill.
  After flush + light oil (nu ~25 at room temperature): 16.2 mN m at 225 rpm, full rotor -> 6-8x less
  Check against 11-22 (ring + plate, oiled): model 8.8 mN m -> 33 mA over the base; measured 165 mA vs a 124-180 mA base
  Small bearings instead of the 6820 (two on a spindle, full rotor on one, the other preloaded 10 N), grease nu 150, 225 rpm:

    2 x 608ZZ: 0.6 + 0.7 = 1.3 mN m (oiled: 0.6)

== G. GT2 belt 20T / 40T, 122 mm, C 30.33
  pitch radii 6.37 / 12.73 mm; free span 29.7 mm; wrap on the 20T 156 deg = 8.7 teeth in mesh
  before (0.125 N m)                   effective tension   9.8 N -> slack side goes slack below T0 = 4.9 N per strand
  after, hub incl. 6802 (0.040 N m)    effective tension   3.1 N -> slack side goes slack below T0 = 1.6 N per strand
  T0  3 N: pluck  337 Hz, 1 N (~100 g) at mid-span deflects 2.47 mm, hub / 6802 side load 6 N
  T0  5 N: pluck  435 Hz, 1 N (~100 g) at mid-span deflects 1.48 mm, hub / 6802 side load 10 N
  T0  8 N: pluck  551 Hz, 1 N (~100 g) at mid-span deflects 0.93 mm, hub / 6802 side load 16 N
  T0 12 N: pluck  674 Hz, 1 N (~100 g) at mid-span deflects 0.62 mm, hub / 6802 side load 24 N
  T0 20 N: pluck  871 Hz, 1 N (~100 g) at mid-span deflects 0.37 mm, hub / 6802 side load 40 N
  Finger tension (rev n diag step 3: ~3 mm under a light push) is T0 ~ 3-5 N: slack below Te 6-10 N. Before, Te was ~10 N:
  the slack side went slack and the 20T's ~9 teeth ratcheted (the pops, 2-3 per turn, count set by the tension).
  'As tight as possible' (T0 >~ 15 N) stopped the skips but put >30 N on a 5 mm-wide 6802 cantilevered 8-10 mm.
